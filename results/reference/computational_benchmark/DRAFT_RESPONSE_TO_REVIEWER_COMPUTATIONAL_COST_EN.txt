Reviewer comment: The computational cost of feature extraction and the practical efficiency of the proposed frozen-feature framework are insufficiently quantified.

Response: We thank the reviewer for this important point. We added an ROI-to-prediction computational benchmark that separates one-time feature extraction, downstream recalibration, and online inference. The complete 594-image ViT extraction required 10.80 s, whereas the hybrid extraction required 49.37 s after including the 32 handcrafted descriptors. Once features were available, selected-hyperparameter fitting required 0.909 s for the frozen ViT–SVM and 0.407 s for the proposed PCA–SVM. Direct batch-size-1 ROI-to-prediction latency was 25.06 and 109.61 ms/image, respectively. We also report peak memory, trainable parameters, checkpoint/model-bundle size, feature-cache storage, and the completed full fine-tuning training cost. The manuscript now explicitly distinguishes the one-time offline extraction overhead from the rapid downstream recalibration benefit and no longer implies that feature extraction itself is cost free.
