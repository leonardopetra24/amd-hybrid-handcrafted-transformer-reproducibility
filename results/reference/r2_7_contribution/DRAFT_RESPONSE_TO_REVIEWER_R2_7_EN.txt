Reviewer R2.7: The proposed fusion mechanism provides limited evidence of meaningful handcrafted-feature contribution. The reviewer requested coefficients where meaningful, permutation importance, family ablation, PCA loading analysis, matched dimensionality, and a 32-dimensional random or generic control.

Response: We thank the reviewer and have substantially expanded the contribution analysis. First, the previously completed leakage-safe family ablation separately evaluated Color, LBP, and Haralick additions and removals. Second, we added a matched-dimensionality diagnostic in which ViT-only, ViT+Random32, ViT+Generic32, and ViT+the exact Handcrafted32 representation were all compressed to 128 dimensions and evaluated using the same locked outer-fold pipeline. Third, we performed grouped permutation importance on held-out outer-fold data before scaler and PCA transformation. Fourth, we decomposed variance-weighted PCA loadings by feature family and reported dimensional-share enrichment. Fifth, because the final classifier is an RBF-SVM and therefore has no direct standardized coefficients in the original input space, we did not manufacture or mislabel nonlinear feature coefficients. Instead, we added a standardized linear-SVM surrogate analysis as a clearly identified supplementary diagnostic.

Across five seeds, the proposed matched representation achieved 93.30% ± 0.28% accuracy and 91.70% ± 0.35% macro-F1. The corresponding ViT-PCA128 control achieved 93.13% ± 0.08% accuracy and 91.51% ± 0.11% macro-F1. Complete paired comparisons, Random32 and Generic32 controls, grouped permutation results, loading tables, surrogate coefficients, fold-level predictions, and confidence intervals are now provided in the supplementary and reproducibility package.

We have also narrowed the manuscript interpretation. PCA loading is discussed only as variance representation, permutation and ablation are treated as predictive diagnostics, and no feature family is described as universally or clinically important.
